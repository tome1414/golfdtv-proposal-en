DTV (Destination Thailand Visa)
Thailand's New 5-Year Long-Stay Visa

The DTV (Destination Thailand Visa) is a 5-year long-stay visa newly established by the Thai government in 2024. Renewable every 180 days, it allows continuous long-term residence in Thailand for up to 5 years.

Eligible purposes: Workation (digital nomads, remote workers, freelancers), Soft Power activities (Muay Thai, Thai cuisine, medical treatment, cultural arts), and Family (spouses/children of DTV holders).
